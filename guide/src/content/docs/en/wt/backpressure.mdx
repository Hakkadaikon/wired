Backpressure on a client-initiated stream: hold its receive credit instead
of reading faster than the app can keep up, then release it. Separately,
abort a server-sent stream the app no longer needs, and check whether one
is still in flight.

<Example id="wt-backpressure" />

## How it works

- The first delta of a client upload (`fin=0`) calls
  `wired_server_wt_stream_hold(s, stream_id, 1)`: the loop stops raising
  the advertised `MAX_STREAM_DATA`, so the peer's already-granted bytes
  still arrive but no more credit is handed out.
- The closing delta (`fin=1`) calls `hold(..., 0)` to resume, then checks
  `wired_server_wt_stream_inflight(s, g_uni)` on the separate server-opened
  stream from `on_session` -- `1` while it is still open or unacknowledged.
- Sending `"abort"` instead calls `wired_server_wt_stream_reset(s, g_uni,
  1)`, which frees that stream's send slot at once: `inflight` reads back
  `0` right after.
- The client sends its first upload as two separate writes before closing,
  so the server sees two reassembly deltas instead of one -- that chunk
  before the final one is what the `hold` waits for.
- `wt_on_stream_data` hands over only the bytes new since its previous
  call, and `fin` can arrive on a call of its own with no bytes. So even
  the single-write `"abort"` round can come as a `fin=0` delta first; the
  example therefore holds only the first upload, and collects each round
  until `fin` before comparing it with `"abort"`.

## When a server-initiated open is refused

`wired_server_wt_open_uni`/`_bidi` (and their `_stream` forms) return -1
when the session has no credit for one more stream: the peer's
`WT_MAX_STREAMS` or `WT_MAX_DATA` (draft-ietf-webtrans-http3 §5), its QUIC
`MAX_STREAMS` (RFC 9000 §4.6), or this session's share of the connection's
send slots. Set `wired_srvrun_opt.wt_on_stream_credit` (and
`wt_stream_credit_ctx`) to learn when to retry: it is called once per
refused open, from the connection's step, as soon as the same open would
succeed. Opening again from inside the callback is allowed; a second
refusal arms it again.

How many sessions one connection may hold is the build-time
`WIRED_SRVRUN_MAX_WT_SESSIONS` (default 2, at least 2). All sessions on a
connection share its WT stream slots, so raise
`WIRED_SRVLOOP_MAX_WT_STREAMS`/`_UNI_STREAMS` with it.
