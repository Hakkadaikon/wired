MoQT Objects can travel over two different wire shapes with different
guarantees. This page sends one Object as a QUIC DATAGRAM (lossy, no retry,
bounded by the connection's own datagram size) and a bigger one as a stream
(reliable, and chunked at a 16 KiB per-Object cap).

<Example id="moqt-delivery" />

## How it works

- `moqdg_put` encodes one OBJECT_DATAGRAM (draft-ietf-moq-transport-22
  11.2.1) directly; `wired_server_wt_send_datagram_to` queues it as a QUIC
  DATAGRAM, no stream and no retransmission involved.
- `moqdata_blob_build` frames a blob as one SUBGROUP_HEADER followed by
  Objects of at most `MOQDATA_BLOB_CHUNK` (16384) bytes each
  (draft-ietf-moq-transport-22 11.3.1). This page's blob is 20000 bytes, so
  it comes out as two Objects: 16384 bytes, then the remaining 3616.
  `wired_server_wt_open_uni` then sends the whole framed stream reliably,
  FIN included.
- The client's `go run` output shows both: the datagram arrives whole as a
  single payload, while the stream arrives as `object_1` and `object_2`
  whose lengths add back up to the original 20000 bytes.
- This page builds the Objects directly with `moqdata`/`moqdg`, bypassing
  `wired_moqt_hub`: there is no PUBLISH/SUBSCRIBE control-plane exchange
  here, just the two WebTransport delivery primitives the hub itself is
  built on. See [Publish a track](../publish/) for the hub's own
  higher-level API.
