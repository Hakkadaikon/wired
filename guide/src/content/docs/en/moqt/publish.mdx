The hub can publish a track of its own. This page publishes `hello` as track
`greeting`; the client SUBSCRIBEs and receives it.

<Example id="moqt-publish" />

## How it works

- `wired_moqt_publish_blob(&hub, name, alias, blob, wire)` frames `blob` into
  `wire` once, at startup. `MOQDATA_BLOB_WIRE_CAP(n)` sizes `wire`, and
  `wire` must outlive the hub.
- A SUBSCRIBE that names the track gets SUBSCRIBE_OK with that Track Alias.
  The framed bytes then go to that peer on a new unidirectional stream,
  through the `send_uni` entry of the io table.
- The stream holds a SUBGROUP_HEADER (Track Alias, Group 0) followed by the
  Object (ID 0, payload `hello`). The hub matches the Track Name only, not
  the namespace.
- Everything else is the MoQT hub page unchanged.
